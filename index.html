COLLATERAL

What to expect during verification

Submitting collateral details does not mean the item has been verified or that a request has been approved.

When you apply

Describe the proposed collateral honestly and provide a reasonable estimated value. Only share information needed to review your request. Keep original ownership documents secure unless an authorised staff member explains a safe verification process.

During verification

CampusCash staff may arrange a physical check and update the status after review. Ask for clarification if you are unsure what is required, and do not hand over property or pay a fee based on an unverified message.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

What amount can I request?

The form accepts requests from MWK 20,000 to MWK 2,000,000. The amount requested is not a promise of approval or disbursement.

How is the estimate calculated?

It is a flat, one-time estimate: interest equals principal multiplied by the selected rate; total repayment equals principal plus interest. Rates shown are 20% for 1 week, 30% for 2 weeks, 40% for 3 weeks and 50% for 4 weeks. No compounding is included.

Will every application be approved?

No. Applications are subject to review and collateral verification. A final decision and any approved terms must be communicated before you decide whether to accept.

How do I check collateral or application status?

Sign in to your student dashboard. If you need clarification or your status is not clear, contact support using the details below.

Does submitting the form send an email?

The website does not claim to send application emails. Registration confirmation emails depend on the Supabase authentication email configuration.
