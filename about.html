ABOUT US

Helping students access opportunity with confidence.

CampusCash Malawi was created to make financial support easier for students who need a small, transparent loan to continue their education.

Our mission

We aim to provide a secure and student-friendly platform for education financing while keeping communication clear and approachable.

How it works

  • Create a student account.
  • Submit your application and purpose.
  • Complete collateral verification.
  • Track your application status online.

Why students choose us

We focus on simple steps, clear repayment expectations, and a digital experience that reduces the stress of applying for support.

OUR VALUES

Built with student needs in mind

Transparency

Applicants can see how repayment estimates are calculated before submitting a loan request.

Access

We strive to keep the process accessible, mobile-friendly, and easy to navigate for students.

Support

CampusCash staff review applications and arrange verification steps to guide students through the process.
