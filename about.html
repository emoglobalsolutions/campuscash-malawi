ABOUT CAMPUSCASH MALAWI

Education support should start with clarity.

CampusCash Malawi is a digital student-financing platform intended to help students submit a request, understand an illustrative repayment estimate and track the review process. Applications are assessed individually; submitting a request does not guarantee approval or disbursement.

WHY WE CREATED THE PLATFORM

A more understandable route to student support

Students can face urgent education-related costs and find it difficult to understand where to start. CampusCash Malawi was created to bring the initial request, estimate and progress updates together in one digital experience.

The platform is a way to submit and track a request—not a guarantee of available funds, approval, a particular timeline or a lending outcome. Applicants should review all final written terms and ask questions before accepting any offer.

THE PROCESS

How student financing requests work

Each application is reviewed; steps and decisions may depend on the information provided.

01

Register securely

Create an account using your email. If email confirmation is enabled, complete that step before signing in.

02

Submit details

Provide student, education, contact, purpose and requested-loan details through the application form.

03

Collateral review

Share a description and estimated value. Staff may arrange a physical verification; submission is not verification.

04

Review and track

Review status updates in your dashboard. Before accepting any offer, check the approved amount, all charges and due date in writing.

OUR COMMITMENT

Transparency and responsible lending

We want students to have the information to ask questions and make a considered choice.

Clear estimates

The calculator shows the principal, selected one-time flat rate, interest and total estimate. Interest = principal × rate; total = principal + interest. It does not compound interest.

Review final terms

An estimate is not an offer or approval. Confirm the final approved amount, repayment schedule, due date and every applicable charge before accepting.

Borrow thoughtfully

Consider whether repayment is manageable for you. Ask for an explanation of any term you do not understand and do not proceed under pressure.

COLLATERAL VERIFICATION

Student application and verification

At application

Enter accurate details, select a request amount and repayment period, and describe proposed collateral honestly. Do not send passwords, account recovery codes or unnecessary sensitive documents through informal channels.

Verification and decision

Authorised staff review collateral details and may arrange a physical check. The platform records the status; only a properly completed review can establish verification. Ask staff to explain how information or property will be handled before proceeding.

HELP CENTRE

Frequently asked questions

What does CampusCash Malawi do?

It provides a digital platform to register, submit student financing requests and track recorded application status. A submitted request does not guarantee a loan.

What are the rates shown by the calculator?

The published calculator uses flat one-time rates: 1 week 20%, 2 weeks 30%, 3 weeks 40%, and 4 weeks 50%. Interest = principal × rate, and total repayment = principal + interest. No compounding or additional fee is included in that calculation.

How much may I request?

The current form range is MWK 20,000–2,000,000. The amount finally approved, if any, may differ and must be confirmed in writing.

How is collateral checked?

Applicants provide collateral details and staff may arrange physical verification. The status remains pending until the verification process is completed and recorded.

Does the site send support emails?

The contact links open your email or WhatsApp application. The website does not claim that a message has been delivered. Supabase registration confirmation depends on the project's email settings.
